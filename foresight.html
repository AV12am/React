How a study is built

Methodology →
  1. IDrivers

    The forces that shape the field — the ones that are certain and the ones that are not — each traced to open sources.

  2. IIScenarios

    The two most uncertain, most decisive drivers set the axes. Each quarter is a coherent future, told as it would unfold.

  3. IIISignposts

    Observable signs that one future is arriving: a law, a price, a vote, an appointment. Named in advance.

  4. IVWatch

    The signposts are tracked. When they move, the study is revised and the change is dated — the earlier versions stay.
