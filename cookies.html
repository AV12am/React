In short

The public site sets no cookies and uses no analytics, advertising or tracking. It keeps a few settings in your browser’s local storage so that the pages work as you left them. Nothing in it is sent to us.

What is stored

WhereKeyWhat forHow long
CONTEXT, CONTEXT Newscontext-news-langThe language you chose for the digest (Ukrainian or English)Until you clear it
SITUATIONrc.situation.v1Your dashboards and widgetsUntil you delete them or clear it
Workspace (members)Sign-in sessionKeeping you signed in; strictly necessary for the serviceUntil you sign out or the session ends

Your choice

Because this storage is needed for the features you use and is not used to track you, no consent banner is shown. You can remove it at any time in your browser’s settings (site data for reactioncore.uk). The site keeps working; your settings and dashboards will be gone.

Third parties

Pictures in the CONTEXT digest are loaded from the news outlets that published them; those outlets may set their own cookies under their policies. See Privacy, section 5.
