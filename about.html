ABOUT

Open sources, one picture.

REACTIONcore is an independent analytical platform that works with open sources. We analyse academic research, official documents, registries, financial statements, media and social networks. Every source is checked and rated for reliability. On this basis we prepare research and forecasts using our own methodology: we model how events may unfold, identify the points where scenarios converge and estimate the probability of each outcome.

Every forecast carries a numerical estimate and a date to check it against. We publish the accuracy of our forecasts openly.
