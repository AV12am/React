3. Data that stays in your browser

SITUATION dashboards and the language you choose for the CONTEXT digest are kept only in your browser’s local storage. They are not sent to us. You can remove them by clearing the site’s data in your browser. See the Cookies page.

4. Who processes data for us

  • Vercel — hosting of the site and its server functions.
  • Resend — delivery of the Careers request to our mailbox.
  • Supabase — the Workspace database and sign-in.
  • Cloudflare R2 — storage of files added in the Workspace.

Some of these providers are based in, or use servers in, the United States. Where data leaves the UK or the EEA, we rely on the safeguards the law provides, such as standard contractual clauses or an adequacy decision.

5. Content from other sites

The CONTEXT digest shows pictures from the news outlets it quotes and links to their articles. When your browser loads such a picture, or you follow a link, that outlet receives your IP address under its own privacy policy.

6. Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, to restrict or object to its use, and to receive it in a portable form. Where we rely on consent, you can withdraw it at any time — this does not affect what was done before. Write to privacy@reactioncore.uk; we reply within one month.

You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority — in the United Kingdom, the Information Commissioner’s Office (ico.org.uk); in the EU, the authority of your country; in Ukraine, the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights.

7. Security

Access to the Workspace requires a passkey, connections are encrypted, and access is logged. No system is completely secure; if a breach affects your data, we will tell you and the authority as the law requires.

We will update this page when our practice changes and show the date at the top.
